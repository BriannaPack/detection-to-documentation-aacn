Learn the tool

Read a document’s history

Start with Google Docs’ built-in version history to see who wrote each part and when. Then open the extension’s Details to add how it was written: keystrokes, sessions, writing time, and pastes.

Try it

Open a Google Doc you’ve written over a few sittings. Click the clock icon at the top right to open Version history, then click Details in the Revision History bar.

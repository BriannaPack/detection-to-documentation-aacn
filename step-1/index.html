Learn the tool

Install the extension

Add the free ‘Revision History’ extension to Chrome, pin it, and sign in, so you can see the history of your own Google Docs.

Try it

In Chrome, go to revisionhistory.com and click Add to Chrome. Pin it to your toolbar, open any Google Doc, and click Sign in with Google in the bar at the top.
